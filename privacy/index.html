Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Your wedding information

Posaly stores the wedding name, date, venue, guest names, RSVP responses, meal preferences, dietary notes, seating assignments, vendor contact details, contract amounts, payments, actual spending, schedule records and notes that you enter. Add only information you have permission to use. These records remain in the app’s private storage on your device.

Photos and camera

With your permission, you can capture contract pages and wedding scene photos or choose photos from your library. Posaly saves a compressed copy in private app storage. Generating a booklet includes the photos you attached to its records. Saving a completed booklet to Photos creates separate copies in your photo library.

Voice and speech

Microphone and speech permissions are requested only when you start Voice Input. Posaly records a temporary audio file and requests on-device English speech recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can type instead. Temporary audio is deleted after recognition or cancellation. Review the text before saving it to your record.

Purchases and device services

Apple processes payments. Posaly receives verified purchase identifiers and product references to maintain your Run balance and per-section creation allowances and prevent duplicate delivery. Posaly does not receive your full payment-card information. Notifications use Apple’s permission system. You can change permissions in the device Settings app.

Sharing and third-party services

Posaly does not send wedding records, photos or voice recordings to a developer-operated server or third-party analytics service. Apple provides payment, permission and device services under its own policies. You choose whether to allow photo-library access and whether to save booklet pages outside Posaly. Remote privacy and terms pages load over HTTPS; their hosting provider may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address and request time.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on this installation until you delete them, clear app data or remove the app. Clear All Data deletes the wedding records, media and saved booklets and removes unused passes after two confirmations. Minimal processed purchase identifiers remain in the app’s private store to prevent previously delivered purchases from being granted again. Copies you saved to Photos are not deleted by Posaly. Device backups may be managed by your operating system and account settings.

Your choices

Editing, photos, voice input and purchase actions are optional. Permission denial does not prevent typed planning. You can edit or remove records within their pages. Export any booklet you wish to keep before clearing your data. Do not use Posaly as the only copy of irreplaceable documents.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact posaly.wedding.help@gmail.com. This policy applies to Posaly, bundle com.posaly.app. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
